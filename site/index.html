Chess notation tools — powered by cheesse WASM

  • Autoplay — two random AIs play each other
  • Parser — paste a game in any notation, step through the boards
  • Converter — convert between Algebraic, Descriptive, ICCF, Smith, Coordinate, Figurine
  • Play — play against the AI (Easy / Medium / Hard)
